🌍 Our Story

Built to Fight Misinformation

TruthLens is a final-year university project born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to the truth.

🎯 Our Mission

Empowering the Informed Citizen

We live in an era of information overload. Misinformation spreads faster than truth, and the average person lacks the tools to tell the difference.

TruthLens was built to change that. By combining the power of Google Gemini, The Guardian's journalism, and Wikipedia's encyclopaedic depth, we've created a verification pipeline that anyone can trust.

Our goal isn't just to label claims — it's to explain why, so you become a more critical news consumer over time.
